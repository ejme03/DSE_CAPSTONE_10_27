# Privacy Policy **DSE Capstone – Wearable Data Integration Proof of Concept** **Effective Date:** November 9, 2026 ## 1. Project Overview This application is being developed as part of an academic capstone project at the United States Military Academy (USMA). The project evaluates the technical feasibility of integrating wearable physiological data into a prototype decision-support system. The application is intended solely for academic development and proof-of-concept testing. It is not an operational medical or health-monitoring system. ## 2. Voluntary Participation Participation is voluntary. Participating cadets may authorize access to their WHOOP data through WHOOP's authentication process. Participants may revoke the application's access to their WHOOP accounts. Revoking authorization prevents future authorized retrieval but does not automatically delete previously collected information. ## 3. Data Collection and Use With participant authorization, the application may retrieve WHOOP metrics including: - Recovery scores and heart rate variability - Resting heart rate and other available heart rate metrics - Sleep duration and performance - Physical activity and strain information Collected data will be used exclusively to demonstrate automated data retrieval, processing, analysis, and visualization capabilities. The project does not intend to include participant names or direct WHOOP account identifiers in its analytical dataset. ## 4. Participant Identification Participants will be assigned coded identifiers in place of their WHOOP account identifiers within the analytical dataset. This approach is intended to reduce the use of directly identifying information while allowing measurements to be associated with the same participant over time. Coded data may remain re-identifiable and will not be treated as fully anonymous. ## 5. Data Storage and Security Project data will be stored using Microsoft Azure services. Access to collected data will be limited to authorized capstone team members and project sponsors. The project plans to implement authentication and access controls to protect collected information. These measures must be established before live participant data is stored. ## 6. Data Sharing Collected data will be used for project development, testing, and evaluation by authorized capstone team members and project sponsors. Leader-facing demonstrations will use de-identified demonstration data. Data will not be sold or used for advertising. No additional recipients are intended beyond the authorized project team, sponsors, and service providers necessary to operate the prototype. ## 7. Data Retention and Deletion Collected project data will be retained until completion of the academic capstone project. Upon completion, collected project data will be deleted, including applicable stored copies and backups. Participants may request deletion of their collected data by contacting the project team. ## 8. Changes to This Policy This policy may be updated to reflect changes in project scope or data-handling practices. Updates will be published on this page. ## 9.
